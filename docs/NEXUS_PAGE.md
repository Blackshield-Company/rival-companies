## Description (paste into the BBCode editor)

```bbcode
[center][size=6][b]RIVAL COMPANIES[/b][/size]

[size=3][color=#e0a030][b]EARLY BETA (v0.1.0)[/b]: this version passes every automated build check but hasn't been playtested in game yet. Please report bugs in the Bugs tab or on GitHub, ideally with your Papyrus log.[/color][/size][/center]

[line]

[size=4][b]Summary[/b][/size]
Four rival mercenary companies have pitched camp across Skyrim. Raid a camp for its loot and the bounty on its leader, or win the company over and recruit its veteran.

Base game only. No SKSE. ESL-flagged. Edits no vanilla records or cells.

[size=4][b]The companies[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]The Ashen Lances[/b], near Solitude Lighthouse. Captain Hrodna Ash-Spear; veteran Ulfgar the Lance.
[*][b]The Red Tithe[/b], by Stony Creek Cave in Eastmarch. Varro Teneus; veteran Sela Coin-Sworn.
[*][b]The Gallows Pikes[/b], at Brittleshin Pass in Falkreath. Morvayn Gallows-Hand; veteran Brother Kesh.
[*][b]The Thornwatch[/b], below Stendarr's Beacon in the Rift. Ilvie Thorn-Captain; veteran Dagny Thornblade.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Raid or win over[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Raid:[/b] tell the leader the company ends here, or just draw blood. The whole camp fights, the chest is free to loot, and the leader carries a bounty note.
[*][b]Persuade:[/b] Speechcraft 50 or better.
[*][b]Tribute:[/b] 500 gold.
[*][b]Blackshield:[/b] if Blackshield Company is installed and you hold a rank, its name opens doors.
[*][b]Duel:[/b] one on one, to bleedout. The leader can't die mid-duel and the rest of the camp stays out.
[/list]
Win a company over and its veteran becomes a free vanilla follower. One veteran per company, no wages, no roster.

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
Skyrim Special Edition or Anniversary Edition (1.5.97 or 1.6.x). Nothing else. No SKSE, no DLC.

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
Install with a mod manager (FOMOD). Enable RivalCompanies.esp. Load order doesn't matter.

[size=4][b]Settings[/b][/size]
[code]
set RVC_PersuadeSkill to 50
set RVC_TributeGold to 500
[/code]

[size=4][b]The series[/b][/size]
Part of the Blackshield series. These camps are not your camp (that's Company Quartermaster) and this is not hiring (that's Sellsword Roster).

[url=https://github.com/Blackshield-Company/rival-companies]Source and issues[/url].

Donations are optional. Nothing is gated.
```
